HT Leadership Summit 2026 — New Rules. New Realities.

About the Summit

As the world enters an era defined by rapid disruption and unprecedented change, the 24th edition of the Hindustan Times Leadership Summit turns its focus to the forces rewriting the way we live, lead and shape the future. The theme, “New Rules. New Realities.”, captures a world where old assumptions are being challenged and new possibilities are emerging.

Across economies, geopolitics, technology, business and society, the rules of engagement are changing. Artificial intelligence is transforming industries and human potential; shifting geopolitical alliances are redefining the global order; new economic realities are reshaping markets; and changing social and cultural dynamics are influencing how the world thinks, works and connects.

HTLS 2026 will bring together influential voices from politics, business, technology, economics, sports, entertainment, science and global thought to examine these new realities and the forces behind them. The conversations will explore what is changing, what it means for India and the world, and how leaders can navigate a landscape where certainty is increasingly being replaced by adaptation.

For over two decades, the Hindustan Times Leadership Summit has been a platform where leaders and ideas come together to shape the discourse of our times. In its 24th edition, HTLS continues that legacy—bringing together diverse perspectives to understand the new rules, decode the new realities, and imagine what comes next.
